## Doctrines

A doctrine (`.lore/doctrines/`) is a directory of prose for a standard body of work: `<id>.design.md` for the
orchestrator and one `missions/<mission-id>.md` for each worker. Lore parses none of it; you read the design and
decide the order. Lore's own doctrines sit under `default/`; put yours beside it.
