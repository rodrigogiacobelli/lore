feature: quests
commands: [board add, needs, unneed, list, missions, stats, oracle]
---
Pass notes between missions with `lore board add`. Declare order with `lore needs <a>:<b>` (a waits for b) and
undo it with `lore unneed`. `lore list`, `lore missions` and `lore stats` show state; `lore oracle` writes reports.
