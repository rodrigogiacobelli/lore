**Worker**
- You are given a mission id. `lore show <id>` returns the description, acceptance criteria and the doctrine
  instructions in one call.
- Execute, then `lore done <id>`; if stuck, `lore block <id> "<reason>"`.
- Do not create quests or missions and do not claim unassigned work.
