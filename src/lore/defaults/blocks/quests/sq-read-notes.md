Note the phases and their order; the mission ids, titles, types (`agent`, `constable`, `human`) and depends-on edges from
the design table; and, if the user asked for a trimmed run, which missions to include and which to skip. You do not need to
read every mission body to plan the quest: the design table carries the workflow, and the worker receives its mission body
automatically through `-D`.
