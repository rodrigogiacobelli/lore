If the change adds or modifies an entry in `.lore/codex/glossary.yaml`, first ask whether it is a glossary term at all: most
term-like additions belong in an entity, workflow or decision document instead. A term is one entry in one file, with no
reference of its own; `keyword` is the identity, so renaming a term is a delete plus a new one.
