### 2. Collect the fields

For each custom field gather its **name** (the frontmatter key), its **type** (`string`, `integer`, `number`, `boolean`,
`array` or `object`, plus any small constraint such as `minLength: 1`, `format: date` or `enum`), and whether it is
**required** on every doc of this kind.
